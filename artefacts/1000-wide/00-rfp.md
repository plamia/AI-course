# EU Machinery Corp — ERP Modernisation & AI Sales-Ops RFP

## 1. Buyer & Decision
The buyer is **EU Machinery Corp**, a €600M European industrial-machinery manufacturer with 1,200 employees across 9 EU operational sites. 

The decision EU Machinery Corp is making is to select a single system integration partner to retire and replace its 14-year-old custom ERP-integration layer, deliver a customer self-service portal, and deploy an AI-powered sales-operations assistant (quote generation, lead triage, and order-status automation).

## 2. Objective
EU Machinery Corp requires a unified, scalable digital integration backbone and customer portal to modernize field and customer interaction while accelerating sales responsiveness. Success is defined as:
1. Complete retirement of legacy middleware before contract expiration in 12 months with zero lost transactions.
2. A secure customer portal providing real-time order visibility across all 9 manufacturing sites.
3. An AI sales-ops assistant reducing routine quote generation time by 50% while operating in full compliance with EU data protection requirements.

## 3. Scope

### In Scope
- **ERP Integration Layer:** Re-architecting and replacing legacy custom integration scripts with a modern cloud-native integration architecture.
- **Customer Self-Service Portal:** Web interface for B2B customers to view order status, request spare parts, and submit service tickets.
- **AI Sales-Ops Assistant:** Generative AI / Agentic capabilities for automated quote draft generation, incoming B2B sales lead triage, and dynamic order-status Q&A.
- **Data Migration & Systems Integration:** Integration with core Azure tenant services, legacy ERP backend, and existing CRM.
- **Deployment & Knowledge Transfer:** Production rollout across 9 EU sites and training for core operations staff.

### Out of Scope
- Core ERP system replacement or database schema re-engineering (the underlying ERP remains intact).
- Direct hardware or infrastructure provisioning outside the existing Microsoft Azure tenant.
- B2C e-commerce capabilities or public consumer-facing marketing platforms.
- Multi-vendor consortium management (the winning supplier must take single-point responsibility).

## 4. Constraints
- **Timeline:** Hard limit of **12 months** from contract award to full production go-live (driven by fixed legacy maintenance contract expiration).
- **Commercial Model:** Fixed-price commercial model for primary milestone deliverables preferred.
- **Regulatory & Compliance:** Strict compliance with **GDPR** and the **EU AI Act**. All data must remain within EU sovereignty boundaries.
- **Technology Stack:** Must deploy natively on EU Machinery Corp's existing **Microsoft Azure** enterprise tenant.
- **Sourcing:** Single prime contractor model. Subcontracting permitted only with prior approval and direct single-point accountability.

## 7. Submission Rules
1. **Format:** Electronic PDF format only. Spreadsheet data (staffing, cost models) must accompany the response in editable Excel/CSV format.
2. **Page Limit:** Main proposal body must not exceed **25 pages** (excluding team CVs, reference letters, and standard compliance certificates).
3. **Channel:** Submissions must be uploaded directly to the secure EU Machinery Corp Procurement Portal before the Day 30 deadline.
4. **Disqualification Conditions:**
   - Any proposal received after Day 30, 17:00 CET.
   - Proposals relying on multi-vendor joint bids without a single prime contractor holding full contractual liability.
   - Failure to confirm data residence within EU Azure regions.
   - Submissions attempting to modify the hard 12-month delivery constraint without explicit prior approval.
