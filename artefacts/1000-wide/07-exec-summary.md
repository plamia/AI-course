## 1. What We Propose
EPAM proposes a complete, risk-managed replacement of EU Machinery Corp's 14-year-old custom ERP integration layer with a modern, cloud-native Azure Integration backbone (API Management, Logic Apps, Service Bus). In parallel, we deliver a responsive B2B Customer Self-Service Portal for real-time order visibility across all 9 EU sites and deploy an AI Sales-Ops Assistant using Azure OpenAI and the DIAL framework. The AI assistant automates B2B quote drafting and lead triage while operating under strict EU AI Act and GDPR compliance controls.
