# Market & Trend Scan — Italian Consumer Electronics

## Playground
Industry: Omnichannel consumer electronics retail, focused on click-and-collect orders.
Region: Italy — Meridian's online channel and physical stores.
Revenue band: USD 5–10B annually at group level (USD 8.2B in the case); Italian segment revenue is not provided.
Regulatory pressure: GDPR for personal data and PSD2 Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) for applicable electronic payments.
Dominant business problem: Approximately 7% of click-and-collect orders are cancelled at pickup due to phantom stock in the reference case; the rate for Italian consumer electronics requires validation.

## Market Trends
1. **Subdued demand:** Cautious Italian consumers limited non-essential electronics spending, contributing to low retail volume growth in 2025. [S1]
2. **Supply-chain reassessment:** Dependence on imported hardware and geopolitical uncertainty prompted retailers and brands to rethink supply chains and strategies. [S1]
3. **Expected price and inventory instability:** Euromonitor anticipated instability under these conditions. This is a 2025 outlook, not a measured stock-accuracy result or verified September 2026 condition. [S1]

## Competitive Moves
On 26 January 2026, Fnac Darty, which acquired Unieuro, reaffirmed its “Beyond everyday” 2030 objectives. It attributed market outperformance to its omnichannel and service-oriented strategy and identified Italy as a contributor to expected growth in Rest-of-Europe operating income. These are company-reported statements accompanying preliminary, unaudited results; no Italy-specific pickup benchmarks are provided. [S2]

## Regulatory Shifts
On 27 November 2025, the Council and Parliament reached a provisional payment-services agreement covering anti-fraud measures, transparency of card-service fees charged to merchants, and consistency between merchants' trading names and bank-statement names. Technical work and final adoption were still pending. The announcement does not establish the package's legal status or application dates in September 2026. [S3]

GDPR and PSD2/SCA are existing constraints in the reference case. The retained research establishes neither a GDPR shift nor a specific SCA change.

## Three Strategic Pain Points
1. **Inventory planning under uncertainty:** Import dependence and expected inventory instability complicate planning. Hypothesis: these pressures increase the importance of reliable stock promises for Meridian; S1 does not establish phantom stock or explain its cancellations. [S1; reference case]
2. **Competitive service differentiation:** Fnac Darty's stated omnichannel strategy creates a competitive reference point. Hypothesis: reliable pickup could help Meridian differentiate; no competitor pickup-performance gap has been established. [S2]
3. **Payment-change readiness:** The provisional agreement signals potential changes affecting merchants and payment providers. Hypothesis: Meridian should assess checkout and provider readiness; implementation deadlines remain unverified. [S3]
