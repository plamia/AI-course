## 3. Re-Rating the Strategic Pain Points

### P1 — Inventory planning under uncertainty
**Rating: Sharpened — toward fulfilment-status visibility.**

Q2 and Q3 identify uncertainty about order progress and pickup readiness. T3 leaves the communication mechanism unverified. The actionable hypothesis is that timely, consistent status information could reduce customer uncertainty. The evidence does not establish phantom stock, inventory-planning failure or a cancellation rate. **[Q2; Q3; T3]**

### P2 — Competitive service differentiation
**Rating: Sharpened — from offering pickup to dependable execution.**

Unieuro already describes free pickup and alternative payment routes, so merely adding those features is not a demonstrated differentiator. Q1 and Q3 instead suggest investigating proactive updates and service recovery. No superiority of Meridian's service has been established. **[Q1; Q3; T1; T2; T3]**

### P3 — Payment-change readiness
**Rating: Insufficient evidence — original hypothesis retained.**

T2 identifies separate online-payment and in-store-payment routes relevant to a future assessment. It does not demonstrate authentication behaviour or readiness for regulatory change. None of the quotes tests this pain; neither confirmation nor contradiction is justified. **[T2]**

## Evidence Limitations

- Reviews are self-selected negative accounts, not independently corroborated incidents or representative performance statistics. Individual review permalinks were not supplied.
- Q2 is from 2022, outside Kata 1.2's research window; it is historical context only.
- Q1 and Q3 concern adjacent appliance categories. Q2 concerns consumer electronics but home delivery. Transfer to Italian electronics pickup remains a hypothesis.
- Specific transaction locations are not established by the excerpts.
- The supplied pickup-page text is truncated in one place. Missing information cannot be assumed absent from the full page.
- Public instructions do not establish physical stock accuracy, successful fulfilment or legal compliance.
- The payment-readiness pain remains untested rather than receiving a forced rating.
- A five-minute walkthrough duration and the series-level Deep Research run have not been documented.
