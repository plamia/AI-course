# 3. Opportunity

Test whether earlier warnings enable timely intervention. Differentiate through dependable pickup execution, not merely offering collection. Customer demand and incremental benefit still require validation.

<!--
Speaker note:

---

# 4. Solution

Flag orders likely to miss pickup deadlines. Show supporting evidence. Staff verify and act; customers receive confirmed updates. No autonomous inventory changes, refunds or promises.

<!--
Speaker note:
Begin in shadow mode before testing staff-assisted intervention. Compare the predictor with simple order-age and stock-sync rules. Prefer existing capabilities or rules if custom ML adds insufficient value.
-->

---

# 5. Value

Target ≥20% relative reduction in late-readiness versus simple rules, without increasing handling minutes per order. Proposed success threshold—not an observed result.

<!--
Speaker note:
Measure against original recorded pickup deadlines. Do not reset deadlines or remove post-allocation cancellations to improve results. The pilot must establish whether staff action—not prediction alone—improves fulfilment.
-->

# 7. Risks

Binding data gate: ≥90% of 200 orders must have linked deadlines and outcomes. ML must beat rules; staff intervention must improve fulfilment without added labour.
